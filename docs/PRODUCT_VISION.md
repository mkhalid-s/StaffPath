# StaffPath Product Vision

StaffPath is not only an engineering encyclopedia. It is a local-first, end-to-end Staff Software Engineer preparation system and long-lived source of truth.

## Product promise

One application should help a candidate:

1. Understand the Staff role and assess current gaps.
2. Build and execute a realistic preparation plan.
3. Learn technical, product, leadership, and communication concepts.
4. Study worked solutions and architecture diagrams.
5. Practice system design, AI design, problem solving, SDLC, and people scenarios.
6. Run structured mock interviews and receive actionable scorecards.
7. Record mistakes, feedback, evidence, and spaced-review items.
8. Build a living handbook and reusable interview story bank.
9. Evaluate readiness using gates based on demonstrated performance.
10. Run a final revision plan and preserve the knowledge after the interview.

## Product layers

### Guide

The 90-day roadmap and adaptive daily executor turn preparation into focused one-hour sessions.

### Encyclopedia

A searchable source of truth connects concepts, patterns, failure modes, metrics, systems, and interview questions. Every full chapter uses a consistent schema:

- Problem statement
- Interview question
- Core concepts
- Architecture diagram
- Worked solution
- Design patterns
- Trade-offs
- Failure scenarios
- Production considerations
- Staff Engineer discussion
- Related topics
- Real-world systems
- Follow-up interview questions
- Cheat sheet
- Flashcards
- One-minute interview answer

### Practice and assessment

Practice Labs, timed mocks, rubrics, progressive hints, failure injection, and readiness gates turn passive knowledge into demonstrated skill.

### Handbook and evidence

The application retains reflections, solutions, diagrams, ADRs, technical proposals, STAR stories, mock results, mistake history, and workplace evidence as a portable handbook.

### Assistant

The local assistant uses only the learner's curriculum, encyclopedia chapters, attempts, mistakes, and evidence as context. It recommends review and retrieves curated guidance without fabricating completion, readiness, or unsupported open-ended answers. A future remote AI adapter may add rubric-based critique only through explicit opt-in.

## Non-negotiable design principles

- One-hour daily scope remains realistic.
- Practice and evidence matter more than content volume.
- Company-specific interview packs remain optional overlays.
- Worked solutions show alternatives and trade-offs rather than one “correct” answer.
- Diagrams are versioned, editable, exportable, and connected to solutions.
- Existing learner data remains portable and backward compatible.
- The application remains useful without an account or network connection.
- External sync and AI assistance are optional adapters, not core dependencies.

## Completion definition

The product is complete when a learner can move from initial assessment to a readiness decision without needing a separate tracker, handbook, flashcard tool, mock scorecard, diagram archive, or revision planner.
