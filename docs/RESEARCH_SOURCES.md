# Research Sources and Editorial Policy

StaffPath uses primary or authoritative references for technical and company-specific claims. Sources provide guidance; the application converts that guidance into original explanations, exercises, and assessment rubrics.

## Staff engineering

- [StaffEng guides](https://staffeng.com/guides/) — archetypes, high-leverage work, strategy, technical quality, executive communication and interviewing.
- [Promotion packets](https://staffeng.com/guides/promo-packets/) — evidence, organizational leverage, mentorship, measurable impact and gap tracking.

## Reliability and operations

- [Google SRE books](https://sre.google/books/) — reliability, SLOs, capacity, overload, incidents and production operations.
- [AWS Well-Architected Framework](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/framework/welcome.html) — operational excellence, security, reliability, performance, cost and sustainability review dimensions.

## Delivery and organizational performance

- [DORA capabilities](https://dora.dev/capabilities/) — continuous delivery, loosely coupled teams, culture, customer feedback, platform engineering and delivery performance.
- [DORA continuous delivery](https://dora.dev/capabilities/continuous-delivery/) — testing, deployment automation, security, database changes, observability and fast feedback.

## Security

- [OWASP Threat Modeling Project](https://owasp.org/www-project-threat-modeling/) — the four-question threat-modeling loop and maintained references.
- [OWASP Developer Guide](https://owasp.org/www-project-developer-guide/release/design/threat_modeling/) — data flows, threats and mitigations during design.
- [OWASP Product Security Guide](https://owasp.org/www-project-product-security-guide/) — security throughout requirements, architecture, implementation, verification and operation.

## Architecture

- [Microsoft Azure Architecture Center](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/architecture/) — architecture styles, patterns, reference designs and decision guidance.
- [AWS Well-Architected](https://aws.amazon.com/architecture/well-architected/) — workload review and architecture quality dimensions.

## Company-specific preparation

- [Amazon Leadership Principles](https://www.amazon.jobs/content/en/our-workplace/leadership-principles) — authoritative names and descriptions for an optional Amazon interview pack.

## AI engineering

AI chapters should prefer model-provider documentation, security guidance, and evaluation documentation. They must distinguish general patterns from vendor-specific APIs and include evaluation, data policy, adversarial inputs, fallback, cost, latency and human escalation.

## Editorial checks

Before a chapter is marked complete:

- Technical claims are source-backed or clearly labeled as design judgment.
- Company-specific descriptions use an authoritative public source.
- The solution includes alternatives rather than presenting one universal answer.
- Security, reliability, operations, cost and lifecycle implications are covered.
- Interview advice is separated from production guidance where they differ.
- “Exactly once,” body-language interpretation and other commonly overstated claims are qualified precisely.
- The chapter contains an exercise, solution, diagram, rubric or retrieval practice—not only prose.
